# Source attribution

Use ordinary Markdown links to actual returned page URLs: `[Page title](https://www.notion.so/...)`. Put the link next to the claim it supports. Cite a specific block URL when it is known and helpful; do not manufacture URLs or IDs.

For a quotation preserve the exact wording, identify the source, and keep excerpts proportional to the task. For a paraphrase preserve scope, units and uncertainty. Separate your inference from the source's statement. Record source dates for time-sensitive comparisons and explain conflicts between records rather than silently selecting a convenient value.

Reports can include a compact sources section, grouped by primary evidence and background when useful. Each finding must still be traceable to its supporting page. Do not cite a search-result title as evidence for unread page contents. Missing access or pagination should appear as a specific coverage limitation.
